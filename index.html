Services

Approach

We start on site and work in stages.

  1. Walk the site

    We visit, talk to the crews and the control room, and see how the systems are really used.

  2. Map what runs where

    A clear picture of networks, controllers, servers and data flows, including the ones nobody documented.

  3. Fix in stages

    Small changes timed to shutdowns and shift patterns, each one reviewed for safety before it goes live.

  4. Hand over and stay on call

    Your team gets the documents and the training. We stay available when something needs a second opinion.

Site realities

Mine sites break the usual IT assumptions.

Links drop.
Pits, shafts and ridgelines cut the signal, so systems have to keep working offline.
Shutdowns set the calendar.
Changes land in planned windows, never on a Tuesday afternoon in the middle of production.
Old and new share a site.
A 20-year-old controller sits beside a new telemetry stack, and both have to keep running.
Safety comes first.
Every change gets a risk review before it touches anything that moves, blasts or hauls.
